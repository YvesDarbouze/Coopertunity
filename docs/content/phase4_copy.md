## Prompt 35: About Us (Economic Theory)
**Headline:** From Aid to Enterprise.

**Body:**
Coopertunity is built on a fundamental economic truth: A civilization thrives when it controls its own value chain.

For too long, the African narrative has been one of raw extraction—shipping our wealth out only to buy it back as finished goods. We use the **Four-Sector Economic Model** to visualize and reverse this trend:

*   **Primary Sector (Extraction):** Owning the land, the mines, and the farms.
*   **Secondary Sector (Manufacturing):** processing our own resources into products.
*   **Tertiary Sector (Services):** Distributing and financing our own trade.
*   **Quaternary Sector (Knowledge):** Innovation, technology, and intellectual sovereignty.

Our platform connects the capital and skills of the Diaspora directly to these sectors, bypassing intermediaries and building a closed-loop economy that benefits Africans first. We are not asking for charity. We are facilitating empire.
