# Coopertunity Taxonomy Tree: African Industries

## Philosophy
Classification based on the Classic Economic Sectors:
1.  **Primary**: Extraction of raw materials.
2.  **Secondary**: Manufacturing and processing.
3.  **Tertiary**: Service industry.
4.  **Quaternary**: Knowledge-based sector.

## 1. PRIMARY SECTOR (Extraction)
*Focus: Natural Resources & Agriculture*

*   **Agriculture (Smallholder & Commercial)**
    *   Cash Crops (Cocoa, Coffee, Tea, Cashew)
    *   Staple Foods (Cassava, Maize, Yam, Teff)
    *   Livestock & Pastoralism
*   **Mining (Artisanal & Industrial)**
    *   Precious Metals (Gold, Platinum)
    *   Gemstones (Diamonds, Tanzanite)
    *   Strategic Minerals (Cobalt, Coltan, Lithium)
*   **Energy (Upstream)**
    *   Oil & Gas Exploration
*   **Forestry & Logging**
*   **Fishing & Aquaculture**

## 2. SECONDARY SECTOR (Manufacturing)
*Focus: Value Addition & Infrastructure*

*   **Agro-Processing**
    *   Food Processing & Packaging
    *   Textiles & Garments (Cotton to Cloth)
    *   Leather Works
*   **Construction & Real Estate Development**
    *   Affordable Housing Projects
    *   Road & Bridge Infrastructure
*   **Manufacturing**
    *   Cement & Building Materials
    *   Consumer Goods (FMCG)
    *   Automotive Assembly
*   **Utilities (Downstream)**
    *   Solar Micro-grids & Installation
    *   Water Purification & Distribution

## 3. TERTIARY SECTOR (Services)
*Focus: Distribution & Consumer Services*

*   **Trade & Commerce**
    *   Wholesale & Retail
    *   Import/Export Logistics
*   **Transport & Logistics**
    *   Haulage & Trucking
    *   Ride-hailing & Last Mile Delivery
*   **Hospitality & Tourism**
    *   Eco-Tourism
    *   Hotels & Accommodation
*   **Finance & Banking**
    *   Microfinance & SME Lending
    *   Mobile Money Agents
    *   Insurance
*   **Healthcare Services**
    *   Clinics & Hospitals
    *   Pharmacies

## 4. QUATERNARY SECTOR (Knowledge)
*Focus: Information, Innovation & Strategy*

*   **Technology (ICT)**
    *   Fintech Development
    *   Agritech Solutions
    *   Software Engineering
*   **Education & Training**
    *   K-12 Education
    *   Vocational Training (TVET)
    *   EdTech Platforms
*   **Consulting & Professional Services**
    *   Legal & Compliance
    *   Business Strategy
    *   Security Consulting
*   **Creative Industries**
    *   Film (Nollywood/Afrobeats)
    *   Digital Arts & Design
    *   content Creation
