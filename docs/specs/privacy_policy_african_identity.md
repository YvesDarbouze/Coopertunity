# Data Privacy Policy: "Identify as African"

## Mission Statement
Coopertunity is built on the philosophy of "Africa for the Africans." Collecting ethnic identity data is fundamental to our mission of empowerment and targeted connection. However, we recognize the sensitivity of this data under GDPR, POPIA (South Africa), and other data protection laws.

## 1. Purpose of Collection
We collect the `isAfrican` boolean flag and related heritage data solely for:
1.  **Community Integrity**: Ensuring the platform remains a safe, focused space for the intended demographic to connect.
2.  **Algorithm Optimization**: Prioritizing matches that foster Diasporan-Continental relationships.
3.  **Access Control**: Granting write-access to the platform's core "Coopertunity" posting features.

## 2. Data Logic & User Rights
*   **Self-Identification**: This is a voluntary self-declaration. We do not enforce genetic testing.
*   **Right to Withdraw**: Users can toggle this setting at any time. Toggling "False" will restrict access to "African-Only" features immediately.
*   **Non-Discrimination**: Non-Africans have "Read-Only" access to educational resources and articles, ensuring inclusivity in learning while preserving the community for connection.

## 3. Storage & Security
*   **Encryption**: This specific field is treated as "Sensitive Personal Data" (SPD).
*   **Access**: Not shared with third-party advertisers. Only visible to the User and the Matchmaking Engine.
*   **Anonymity**: Aggregated data (e.g., "40% of users are from the Diaspora") may be used for impact reporting, stripped of PII.

## 4. Consent
By creating an account and toggling "I identify as African," the user explicitly consents to the processing of this special category of data for the specific purpose of community matching.
